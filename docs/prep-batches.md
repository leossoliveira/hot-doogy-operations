# Prep batches and labels

The **Prep batch** workflow (Overview → New activity → Prep batch, or Prep → Add production to inventory) records a completed production run and prints its labels.

## Recording a batch

- **Prep**: a prepared item assigned to the production location, with a count unit and a single active recipe.
- **Production location** and **storage area** follow the existing rules; the quantity is added to reference stock without replacing the physical count and without deducting ingredients.
- **Recorded at** is the moment the batch is saved, in `America/New_York`. There is no editable completion date in this form.
- **Prepared by** is the signed-in account's registered name, read from `GET /api/preparation/preparer` (`operations_current_preparer()`): the HR team member linked to the account, then the profile name, then the account email. The database trigger `prep_batch_preparer` stores the same name on the batch; when the linked team member is active at the production location, the batch is also linked to that team member. Planned batches completed from the batch drawer keep the team-member selector.
- **Labels to print** defaults to the produced quantity rounded up (maximum 500) and can be adjusted before saving.

## Batch numbers and barcodes

Batch numbers and label codes are derived from the batch id and the New York completion date, so labels can be reprinted for batches stored in the database without an extra column:

- Batch number: `BATCH-YYMMDD-NNNNN`, where `NNNNN` comes from the batch id. It identifies the batch in history and in the file name.
- Label batch number: the batch number plus a sequential 3-digit serial (`BATCH-YYMMDD-NNNNN-001`, `-002`, …), unique per label.
- Label code: the 11 digits above plus a 3-digit unit serial (`YYMMDDNNNNNSSS`), unique per label. The barcode is Code 128 (set C), generated by `src/code128.js` with ten-module quiet zones.

## Label PDF

`src/prep-labels.js` generates one 2.4 × 3.5 in page per label in a single PDF (`Hot-Doogy-Prep-Labels-<batch number>.pdf`) in the browser, with no upload or database write. Each label carries the prep item name and its own sequential batch number at the top, then Produced (date and time), Prepared by and Use by (date only when a shelf life is given, otherwise a blank line) spread evenly, and the barcode at the bottom with its human-readable digits. Labels are downloaded right after saving and can be reprinted from the batch drawer or from Prep → Labels.

Tests: `tests/code128.test.mjs` and `tests/prep-labels.test.mjs`.
